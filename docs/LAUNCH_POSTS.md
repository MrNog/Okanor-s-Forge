## Discord

```
# ⚒️ Okanor's Forge is open
Free addons for **WoW 3.3.5a**, forged for my guild **RATS** (Warmane Onyxia, Horde) and now for anyone who raids.

## Okanvil `/okanvil`
The raid and guild toolkit. One window instead of eight addons that don't talk to each other:
- **Loot council** people trust: raiders answer BIS · Upgrade · Small · OS · Pass, the council sees each answer next to what that player wears
- **Mini roll** that runs the roll-off and records the winner
- **Loot history**: every drop, every boss, and how it was given
- **Raid snapshots** at the first pull: groups, real specs, who tanked
- **PuG builder** + **Raid Finder** that reads trade spam for you (B+O+P, GS, roles)
- **Raid notes** on a timer, MRT format, the right note opens in the right room
- Switch off every module you don't use

## RatStash `/rst`
Bags and bank in one window each. Sorted groups that **stay put**, gear sets together, and raid loot kept apart from your own stacks until you say it's yours.

Screenshots, downloads and everything each addon does:
https://mrnog.github.io/Okanor-s-Forge/

Bugs, ideas or questions: come say hi on Discord https://discord.gg/MpwbuGEp69 🐀

## Forum (BBCode)

```
[center][size=6][b]⚒️ Okanor's Forge — free WotLK 3.3.5a addons[/b][/size]
[url=https://mrnog.github.io/Okanor-s-Forge/]https://mrnog.github.io/Okanor-s-Forge/[/url][/center]

Hi all. I'm Okanor, a Holy Paladin and one of the leaders of [b]RATS[/b], a Horde raiding guild on Warmane Onyxia. For the last months I've been building addons for our raid nights, and they've reached the point where other guilds and PuG leaders can use them too. They are free to download and play, and they all live on one site now: Okanor's Forge.

There are two to start with: [b]Okanvil[/b], a raid and guild toolkit, and [b]RatStash[/b], a bag addon.

[size=5][b]Okanvil — one window for the whole raid night[/b][/size]

[b]Why I made it[/b]
Our raid nights ran on a pile of addons, spreadsheets and "who had that again?" on voice. Loot council lived in officer whispers. Boss notes were pasted by hand every week. Building a PuG meant spamming trade and counting healers in my head. None of these tools talked to each other, and half of them were ports that break in odd ways on 3.3.5a. So I started one addon for our guild, and every time something annoyed me on a raid night, it got a new module.

[b]What it does[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Loot Council.[/b] The master looter picks the items and every raider gets a small window: BIS, Upgrade, Small, OS or Pass. The council sees each answer next to what that player is wearing, including the item level gap, and votes. The master looter still decides; votes are advice. There's a test mode that uses your own gear and never hands anything out.
[*][b]Mini roll.[/b] Ask the council or roll, per item. Main spec is /roll 100, off spec /roll 99, and the winner is written down for you.
[*][b]Loot history.[/b] Every drop, every boss, every night, and how it was given: "MS roll 86", "council, BIS", or disenchanted. Speed-run auto master-loot sends each boss's loot to your collector.
[*][b]Raid snapshots.[/b] At the first pull Okanvil records the raid: every group, every player, their real spec (scanned, not guessed) and who tanked. Attendance without a spreadsheet, and one click invites the same roster back next week.
[*][b]PuG builder.[/b] Tank, healer, melee and ranged slots that fill themselves as people whisper. The LFM line rebuilds as you pick raid, size, minimum GS and reserves (BoE, Orbs, Patterns, Fragments...).
[*][b]Raid Finder.[/b] Every LFM in chat becomes one clean row: raid, GS, the roles they still need, whether loot is reserved and what, and whether you're already saved there. It understands the lingo: B+O+P, HR, "3 heals 7 dps".
[*][b]Raid notes.[/b] MRT-format notes per boss, size and difficulty. Timed lines count down in the fight and light up for the player they name. Walk into the room and the right note opens.
[*][b]Recruit.[/b] Your guild advert goes out on its own, "inv" whispers get a guild invite, everything else gets your Discord link, and every conversation lands in an inbox.
[*][b]Small tools.[/b] ID Finder (23,000+ items and spells by name), gold-per-hour farm tracker, raid buff check, raid lockouts of all your characters in one tooltip, and a marks bar.
[/list]
Every module can be switched off per character, so a raider can keep just the loot council and notes while a raid leader runs everything. It stays quiet in combat.

[size=5][b]RatStash — bags that stay where you left them[/b][/size]

[b]Why I made it[/b]
AdiBags sorts well, but its groups move around: a free-space block lands in the middle, your gear sets end up far apart, and the loot you picked up for the raid disappears into your own stacks. Bagnon keeps order but doesn't sort. I wanted both.

[b]What it does[/b]
[list]
[*][b]A fixed order.[/b] Pinned items, then your gear sets, then consumables, professions, gems, and gear at the end. Free space is always last. Nothing reshuffles under your mouse, so you can sell items one after another.
[*][b]Three layouts[/b] per window: one grid, a grid plus a group per gear set, or a block per kind.
[*][b]Raid loot kept apart.[/b] Loot one Primordial Saronite in a PuG and it shows apart from your own six, glowing green with its trade timer, until you say it's yours.
[*][b]Sorting that works in combat[/b], because items are only drawn in order; nothing moves inside your real bags.
[*]Virtual stacks, item level and BoE tags, stack-to-bank, offline bank, and a bag bar.
[/list]
RatStash is still "in the forge": it works and I use it every day, but expect changes.

[size=5][b]Install[/b][/size]
Download the .zip from the addon's page on the site, extract it into World of Warcraft\Interface\AddOns\, and restart the game. Both work on the normal and HD 3.3.5a clients. Turn off other bag addons before using RatStash.
