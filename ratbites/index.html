Free to play · type /bites in game, or click the rat on the minimap

Output and input

Your numbers on their plate, theirs in a box.

Every hit and heal rises over its target's nameplate with the spell's icon, or goes into the Output box. Heals, shields and hits on you go to the Input box.

  • Heals land on the friend you healed, however you cast them: raid frames, mouseover
  • Works with ElvUI plates; hits landing together stack a line apart
  • Boxes move and resize, each with its own scroll direction, alignment and line time
  • The same spell again within a few seconds joins one line: a total with x5
Colours /bites colors

The colour tells you what happened.

Grey hits, gold big hits, orange crits, green heals, mint heal crits, dim overheal, blue shields, red damage taken. Crits get a thick outline and bigger text.

  • Val'anyr, Divine Aegis and Sacred Shield show how much they absorb
  • Auto-attacks show the weapon you have equipped
  • Crits in your class colour, if you like

And the small stuff.

Set it the way you like it.

Five animations

Arc, Rise, Stay, Fountain or Fall, with sideways drift, travel distance and time on screen.

Filters

Normal or critical hits, auto-attacks, spells, damage over time, heals and shields, misses, pet.

Your fonts

Any font your addons share (ElvUI, SharedMedia), with text and icon size.

Game numbers off

The game's own floating numbers turn off while RatBites runs and come back when you disable it.

Test it

/bites test puts one number of each kind on your target; /bites move unlocks the boxes.
